ULTIMATE TIC TAC TOE
The flagship of Tic Tac Verse: 9 boards inside one big board. The cell you mark decides where your rival plays next. Win the small boards to claim cells on the big one and line up three to win. Tic tac toe (XO, noughts and crosses) becomes a real strategy game: easy to learn, hard to master.

6 MORE MODES
• Classic: the traditional 3x3 game with a neon look.
• Shift: only 3 marks per player, the oldest disappears. No boring draws.
• Chaos: random events change the rules mid-match.
• Ultimate Mini: special winning conditions that change every round.
• 4x4: four in a row on a bigger board.
• Five in a row (Gomoku): line up five on a 10x10 board.
